## Basemap data

`md_doc/data/world-110m.json` and `md_doc/data/us-10m.json` (TopoJSON basemaps for the map charts) come from the
[vega-datasets](https://github.com/vega/vega-datasets) package (BSD-3-Clause, Copyright (c) 2015-2023, University of
Washington Interactive Data Lab; the same licence text as above applies), which derives them from Natural Earth
(public domain) and the US Census Bureau (public domain).
